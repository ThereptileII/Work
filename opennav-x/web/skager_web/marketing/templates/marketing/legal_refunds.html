1. Our 14-day promise

You can ask for a full refund of Public Beta Access for any reason within 14 days of the day you bought it. You do not need to explain why, and it does not matter whether you have downloaded or installed SKAGER.

2. How to ask

Open Orders in your account and choose Refund next to your Public Beta Access. Or email {{ operator.email }} from the address you bought with, quoting your receipt. A simple message such as “Please refund my SKAGER purchase” is enough.

3. How you are refunded

We refund the full amount you paid, including any taxes, to the payment method you used, within 14 days of your request. If you used a discount code, we refund the amount you actually paid. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

4. What happens to your access

When the refund is made, your SKAGER account is closed: you can no longer log in, and your access to downloads, updates, the customer portal, SKAGER Cloud and support ends. Any SKAGER Cloud subscription is cancelled at the same time. Your rights under the open-source licences for software you have already received are not affected.

5. Hardware returns

You may return hardware, such as the SKAGER GPS module, within 14 days of receiving it, for any reason. Email {{ operator.email }} with your order details and we will tell you where to send it. Send it back within 14 days of telling us, in its original condition with its accessories.

We refund the price of the product and the standard shipping you paid, within 14 days of receiving the return. You pay the cost of sending it back, unless it is faulty or we sent the wrong item. Hardware you return does not affect your Public Beta Access or your account.

If a product is faulty, contact us at any time within the statutory complaint period (three years in Sweden) and we will repair, replace or refund it.

6. SKAGER Cloud

You can cancel SKAGER Cloud at any time from the Cloud page in your account. It stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and is not renewed. If you change your mind within 14 days of starting the subscription, contact us and we refund the first payment.

7. After 14 days

After 14 days we do not normally refund. Your statutory rights as a consumer still apply: if SKAGER does not work as described, contact us and we will put it right, or refund you where the law requires.

8. Your right of withdrawal

Consumers in the EU have a 14-day right to withdraw from distance purchases. This policy gives you the full 14 days to change your mind even after you start using SKAGER, so you lose nothing by downloading it straight away.
