Is there a subscription?

No. Public Beta Access is a single payment of USD 20. SKAGER Cloud, for automatic updates and online services, is an optional extra at USD 2 a month.

How do I get my account?

It is created when you buy. You confirm your email with a code, choose a password and go straight to your download.

Are charts included?

No. SKAGER uses the charts you already have in OpenCPN. Buy and update charts from your chart provider.

What do I need to run SKAGER?

A 64-bit Windows PC and OpenCPN 5.12.4 for Windows, installed separately. A touch display of 1280 × 800 or larger is recommended at the helm.
