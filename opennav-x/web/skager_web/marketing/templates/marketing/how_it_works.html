How it works

A new interface.
The navigation you trust.

SKAGER sits on top of OpenCPN, the proven open-source chart plotter. OpenCPN does the navigation. SKAGER makes it a pleasure to use.

SKAGERChart-first interface · instruments · traffic · autopilot · energy · weather
OpenCPNCharts · routes · waypoints · AIS · connections
Your boatGPS · AIS · NMEA 0183 · NMEA 2000 · Signal K

Three layers.
One calm screen.

Your instruments talk to OpenCPN exactly as before. OpenCPN keeps your charts, routes and connections. SKAGER brings it all together in an interface designed for the helm.

Getting started

  1. 01

    Install OpenCPN

    Install OpenCPN 5.12.4 for Windows from the official OpenCPN website. Already have it? Check the version and you are set.

    Which version →
  2. 02

    Add SKAGER

    Download SKAGER from your account and run Setup. It checks your OpenCPN, shows what it will change and keeps a backup.

    Installation guide →
  3. 03

    Connect your boat

    Your GPS, AIS and instruments already connected to OpenCPN appear in SKAGER, each with its source and age.

    Connecting instruments →
  4. 04

    Cast off

    Pick a route, check the next turn, glance at the rail. Day, dusk or night, everything you need is on one screen.

    See the features →

Everything you have.
Nothing you lose.

Your charts

The charts, licences and chart plugins you use in OpenCPN work in SKAGER as they are.

Your routes and waypoints

They stay in your OpenCPN profile. Nothing is imported, copied or converted.

Your connections

Set up an instrument once in OpenCPN; SKAGER uses the same connection.

Your way back

Legacy opens the classic OpenCPN interface whenever you want it, and Safe Mode helps if something will not start.
