Chart & passage

More chart.
Less searching.

Your OpenCPN charts with a calm, modern presentation. Switch layers on and off, see the next turn before you reach it, and keep the whole passage in view.

  • Next-turn card with distance, time and new course
  • Route corridor and safety depth along your passage
  • Chart layers you choose: symbols, soundings, contours, AIS, wind
  • North up, course up or head up
{% include 'marketing/includes/visual_layers.html' %}

Traffic

Every ship around you.
One tap away.

AIS targets on the chart with course vectors. Tap one to see who it is, how close it will pass and when, and where the information comes from.

  • Closest point of approach and time to it
  • Onboard AIS first, Online AIS for traffic beyond radio range
  • Source and age on every target
{% include 'marketing/includes/skager_ais.html' %}

Instruments

Know your boat.
At a glance.

Speed, depth, wind and battery in the rail beside the chart, and a full instrument view when you want the detail.

  • Large figures, readable across the cockpit
  • Every value shows its source and age
  • Choose the four values your rail shows
{% include 'marketing/includes/visual_instruments.html' %}

Autopilot

Autopilot.
At your fingertips.

Engage, adjust and hand back the helm from the screen you navigate on. Works with supported pilots once you switch control on.

  • Standby and Auto, ±1° and ±10° steps
  • Every command confirmed by the pilot's own feedback
  • Off until you enable it, and always one tap from Standby
{% include 'marketing/includes/visual_autopilot.html' %}

Energy

Arrive with
energy to spare.

For electric and hybrid boats: charge now, charge on arrival, range and reserve, worked out for the route you are sailing.

  • Estimated battery over the whole passage
  • Your reserve, kept in view
  • Propulsion, battery and motor temperature at a glance
{% include 'marketing/includes/visual_energy.html' %}

Weather

The forecast.
On your chart.

Forecast wind drawn across your route, hour by hour, with its source and time. Measured wind stays separate, so you always know which is which.

Built for trust

Calm, honest,
and always a way back.

Your data stays in OpenCPN

Charts, routes, waypoints and connections stay in your OpenCPN profile. Nothing is copied or converted.

Legacy and Safe Mode

Switch to the classic OpenCPN interface any time, or start with the minimum to sort out a problem.

Honest readings

The whole interface follows the light, so your eyes stay ready for the water.
