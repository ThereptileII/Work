05 / WEATHER IN CONTEXT

The forecast.
On your chart.

Forecast wind direction and speed belong alongside the passage. SKAGER’s developing chart overlay identifies forecast time, source and age, separately from measured wind.

A compatible forecast source, position and coverage are required. Missing coverage stays unavailable. Route weather, waves, currents and tides are future possibilities.
