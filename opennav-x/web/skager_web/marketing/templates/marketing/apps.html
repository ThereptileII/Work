SKAGER Helm · Included with SKAGER

Take the helm.
Anywhere on board.

Your whole SKAGER screen on a phone or tablet, over the boat’s own Wi-Fi. Check the chart, the traffic and the next turn from the cockpit while the PC stays dry below.

  • Pair in seconds: scan the code on the SKAGER screen
  • Tap, swipe and type, just like at the chart table
  • Full-screen chart; the toolbar tucks away until you need it
  • Day, dusk and night, in step with SKAGER
  • Runs on the boat’s Wi-Fi, no internet needed

SKAGER Companion · With SKAGER Cloud

Your boat.
In your pocket.

Ashore, at work or asleep in the forepeak, Companion shows how your boat is doing and tells you the moment something needs you.

  • Position, battery and instruments at a glance
  • Anchor watch on a live map, with a notification if the alarm sounds on board
  • Your voyage track and 30 days of history
  • An alarm log, so you know what happened while you were away
  • Sign in once with your SKAGER account
