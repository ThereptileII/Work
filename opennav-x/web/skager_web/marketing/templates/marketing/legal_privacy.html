3. In the SKAGER application

SKAGER runs on your PC. Your charts, routes, waypoints and tracks stay there. Positions and instrument data stay there too unless you switch on boat data sharing (below).

  • Forecast wind (optional): when you request a forecast, the forecast provider receives the area you asked about and your access token.
  • Online AIS (optional): the AIS provider receives the area you selected and your API key.
  • Linking SKAGER to your account (optional): SKAGER shows a code that you approve in your account. We then store the computer's name, platform and SKAGER version, and when it was last in contact. The link is kept on your PC in Windows Credential Manager. You can unlink it in SKAGER or in your account at any time.
  • Automatic updates (SKAGER Cloud): a linked SKAGER checks for updates with its link, so we can confirm your subscription. It sends the installed version and update channel.
  • Boat data (SKAGER Cloud, off until you switch it on): SKAGER sends your position, speed, course, heading, wind, depth, water temperature, air pressure, rudder and heel, battery, motor and tank readings, each with its age and source, every 15 seconds while it runs. Only you can see them in your account. Simulated and replayed data is never sent.
  • Anchor watch and alarms (with boat data sharing): SKAGER also sends whether its anchor watch is on, the anchor position, radius and distance, and when the anchor alarm or a critical alert starts or ends. If the anchor watch is on and the boat stops reporting for three minutes, we record that contact was lost.
  • Diagnostic bundles are created on your PC and only reach us if you choose to send one.

3a. SKAGER Companion (phone app)

You sign in to SKAGER Companion with your SKAGER account in your phone's browser; the app never sees your password. We store the phone's name and system (Android or iOS), the app version and when it was last used. The app keeps its sign-in in the phone's secure storage and the latest boat data on the phone so it can show it without a connection.

  • Notifications (if you allow them): we store the phone's notification address and send alarms through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) or the Apple Push Notification service (iOS). A notification contains the boat's name and what happened, never its position.
  • Map: map images come from OpenFreeMap, which receives the area the map shows (not your account or the boat's name).
  • Companion only shows your data. It cannot send anything to the boat. You can sign a phone out in the app or in your account at any time.
