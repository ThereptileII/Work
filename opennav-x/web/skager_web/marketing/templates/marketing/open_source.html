Built on shared work

Open source, with credit where it belongs.

SKAGER builds on OpenCPN and the work of its contributors. Applicable open-source rights remain part of the product.

Thank you, OpenCPN

OpenCPN provides the underlying chartplotter and navigation foundation. It is installed separately from SKAGER. SKAGER is an independent product; use of OpenCPN and this credit do not imply endorsement by the OpenCPN project.

Explore the official OpenCPN source repository and the OpenCPN 5.12.4 baseline used by this beta.

Your license rights remain

SKAGER and its dependencies include GPL and other licensed components. Applicable license texts, copyright notices and third-party notices must accompany qualified releases. Paid beta access and service terms do not remove rights granted by those open-source licenses.

Chart-provider licenses remain separate. Licensed private charts are not bundled with SKAGER.

Source that matches your release

Each qualified public release will provide version-specific links to its corresponding source and notice package, tied to the software actually released. These links will be available independently of paid account access. The official upstream repository alone does not supply SKAGER's corresponding source.

Source publication is a release gate

Public downloads remain closed. This preview does not announce a SKAGER release or offer an invented source archive. The exact source mapping, notice package and required compliance review must be complete before public release.
