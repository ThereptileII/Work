Thank you, OpenCPN

OpenCPN is the free, open-source chart plotter and navigation software at the heart of SKAGER. It is developed by volunteers and installed separately from SKAGER. SKAGER is an independent product: it is not affiliated with, and not endorsed by, the OpenCPN project.

Explore the official OpenCPN source repository the OpenCPN 5.12.4 release that SKAGER is built for, and the exact source baseline we build against.

Your rights

SKAGER includes software licensed under the GNU General Public License (GPL) and other open-source licences. Those licences give you the right to run, study, change and share that software, and to receive its source code.

When you buy Public Beta Access, you pay for our services: tested builds, updates, support and your account. Nothing in our terms limits the rights the open-source licences give you. Chart licences from your chart provider are separate.
