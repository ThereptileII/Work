1. Browsing the website

Reading our website sets no cookies, and we use no analytics or advertising tools. That is why you will not see a cookie banner.

2. Signing in to your account

When you sign in, we use three strictly necessary cookies. They cannot be switched off, because sign-in does not work without them, and they are never used for tracking.

CookiePurposeLifetime
__Host-skager_challengeLinks the one-time code we email you to your sign-in attempt10 minutes
__Host-skager_sessionKeeps you signed in20 minutes
__Host-skager_csrfProtects your account forms against forged requests20 minutes

3. Security cookies from our host

If our hosting provider's bot protection is active, it may set a short-lived security cookie, __cf_bm, to tell people from automated traffic. It lasts up to 30 minutes and is not used for tracking.

4. Checkout

When you pay, you are taken to Stripe's checkout page, which uses its own cookies for payment security and fraud prevention under Stripe's privacy policy.
