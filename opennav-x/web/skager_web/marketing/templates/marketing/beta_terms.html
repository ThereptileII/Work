Scope of the beta

SKAGER is a Windows navigation interface built on a separately installed, supported OpenCPN version. The planned public beta remains subject to release qualification. Features, compatibility and known limitations must be identified in the instructions for the exact release.

The supported prerequisite for this preview is OpenCPN 5.12.4 with the Win32/x86 application and plugin interface on a 64-bit Windows PC. Licensed private charts, chart subscriptions and third-party service access are separate.

Navigation and equipment limits

Maintain an appropriate independent means of navigation, current charts and a proper lookout. Inspect data sources, observation ages and quality. Missing, stale, invalid, estimated or uncertain data must not be treated as a current measurement.

SmartNav and energy estimates are advisory. SmartNav never steers. Supplemental Online AIS is not collision avoidance and does not replace onboard AIS, radar or lookout. Unqualified physical outputs remain unavailable or off by default; any separately enabled manual control needs its own commissioning and feedback checks.

Install, update and investigate faults while safely stopped. The complete protected installation and maintenance lifecycle, including the installed updater, still awaits qualification for the public beta.

Feedback and diagnostics

Useful reports describe the version, symptom and reproduction steps. Review any diagnostic file before choosing to share it. Do not include credentials, payment information, private charts or unnecessary navigation data. This preview provides a readable support library; ticket submission and uploads are not enabled.

The final privacy notice must explain any account, support, payment-reference and diagnostic processing before those services begin.

These beta terms must preserve the rights granted by applicable GPL and other open-source licenses. Paid services and account access must not restrict those rights. Qualified public releases will provide corresponding source and applicable notices independently of paid account access.
